⚠️ Scientific and Fiqh Guidelines:
  • Geographical Latitude: Daylight length varies strictly with geographical latitude due to the Earth's axial tilt (23.44°) as it orbits the sun.
  • Daylight vs. Fasting Hours: Fasting (Sawm) begins at true dawn (Subh Sadiq / Fajr) and concludes at sunset (Maghrib). Because astronomical twilight precedes sunrise, fasting durations are approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes longer than visible sunlight.
  • Seasonal Extremes: The Summer Solstice (around June 21) exhibits the longest daylight and longest fast in the Northern Hemisphere, while the Winter Solstice (around December 21) yields the shortest.
